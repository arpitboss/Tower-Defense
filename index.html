HOW TO PLAY

  • 1. Place Towers: Click a tower in the sidebar, then click an empty tile near the path.
  • 2. Upgrade: Select a placed tower to view stats and upgrade options.
  • 3. Send Waves: Click "Send Wave" to start the next assault.
  • 4. Controls: Space = Pause, 1-5 = Select tower type, Esc = Cancel.
  • 5. Survive: Defend the Kingdom Castle for 50 waves!
